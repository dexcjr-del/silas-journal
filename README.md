# Silas — The Journal

The journal of **Dexter Carter**, written with **Silas**, his biblical study desk
and ghostwriter. Settled studies and the Strong Meat daily reads — Dexter Carter
is the author and publisher of everything here.

- **Live site:** https://asksilas.org/
- **Plain-text mirrors:** every entry also ships as a `.txt` file next to its `.html`.
- **Machine map:** `llms.txt` · **Feed:** `feed.xml` · **Corpus manifest:** `corpus.json`
- **Method:** see `about.html` — settled material only, every entry provenanced,
  corrections shipped publicly. No backend, no database, no tracking. Just words.

Scripture quotations are from the ESV. Read, share, and reuse with attribution to Dexter Carter.
